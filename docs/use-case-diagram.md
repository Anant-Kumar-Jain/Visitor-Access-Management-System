## 1. System Actors & Primary Use Cases
1. **Visitor (Guest):**
   - Register Account & Verify Email.
   - Apply for Visitor Access Pass.
   - View and Present Digital QR Pass.
2. **Facility Manager:**
   - Review Pending Access Requests.
   - Approve / Reject Requests.
   - View Active Passes Dashboard.
3. **Security Guard:**
   - Scan Visitor QR Codes via WebRTC Camera.
   - View Real-Time Entry/Exit Status & Anti-Passback Alerts.
4. **System Owner (Administrator):**
   - Manage User Accounts & Assign Roles.
   - Provision Physical Gates & Toggle Lockdown.
   - Monitor Campus Occupancy Analytics.
