## 1. Asset Inventory
- `static/css/style.css`: Master stylesheet containing CSS variables, utility classes, and custom gradients.
- `static/js/html5-qrcode.min.js`: Client-side WebRTC video frame barcode scanning library.
- `static/images/logo.png`: VAMS corporate shield emblem with embedded QR iconography.
- `static/images/favicon.ico`: 32x32 pixel shortcut icon for browser tabs.
- `static/audio/beep-success.mp3`: 100ms high-pitch audio cue indicating approved entry scan.
- `static/audio/beep-denied.mp3`: 300ms double low-pitch audio cue alerting guard of invalid scan or anti-passback violation.
