## 1. Industry Benchmarking Analysis
Audited commercial visitor platforms (Envoy, Proxyclick, Sine):
- **Finding 1:** Digital QR badges on mobile devices reduce check-in queues by 75% compared to paper kiosks.
- **Finding 2:** Instant visual feedback (full-screen green/red color change) is critical for gate guards in noisy, fast-moving environments.
